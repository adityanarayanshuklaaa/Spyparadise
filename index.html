Settings and sources

Photorealistic 3D cities (optional). Paste a free Cesium ion token to unlock world terrain and Google 3D city tiles. The token stays in this browser only.

India cameras (Windy). A free key from api.windy.com/keys adds webcams across India (city, tourist and weather cameras). Their free plan shows a new photo every 5 to 15 minutes, has daily limits, and needs a credit to windy.com, which the app shows. The key stays in this browser.

Public cameras. Live stills from Transport for London (London), the City of Austin, Caltrans (California) and Fintraffic Digitraffic (Finland). The pictures belong to those operators and load straight from their servers. Cameras in other places are not available yet.

Other sources. Flights: adsb.lol and OpenSky Network. Satellites: CelesTrak (orbits computed with satellite.js). Earthquakes: USGS. Imagery: Esri World Imagery. Search: OpenStreetMap Nominatim. Globe: CesiumJS.

Data can be delayed, partial or wrong. Do not use this for navigation, emergencies or any safety decision.

Add a camera

Paste a public link: a YouTube live page, an .m3u8 live stream, an .mp4 video, or a camera photo link. It must start with https. YouTube and .m3u8 links give smooth live video.

Saved only in this browser. Add only cameras whose owners make them public.
